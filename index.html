Our Story

A Love Letter to Rooftop Dining

Born from a simple dream — to create a place where the boundaries between dining and the heavens dissolve — Hotel Happy Stay rose from the heart of Nashik's Swaminarayan Nagar to become the city's most sought-after rooftop culinary destination.

Perched atop our beautifully designed property, our rooftop restaurant is more than just a place to eat — it's an escape. A sanctuary where the warm glow of industrial pendant lights dances with the evening breeze, where lush vertical gardens frame your view of Nashik's glittering skyline, and where every dish tells a story of passion, heritage, and innovation.

Our culinary team draws inspiration from the rich traditions of Maharashtra's wine country, blending time-honored recipes with contemporary flair. Each ingredient is sourced with care, each dish is crafted with precision, and each plate is a canvas of color, texture, and taste that honors the land we call home.

What We Offer

Experiences That Touch The Sky

From intimate dinners under the stars to grand celebrations that light up the night, we craft unforgettable moments tailored to your every desire.

Rooftop Dining

Our signature open-air dining experience under the vast Nashik sky. Enjoy perfectly set tables, ambient pendant lighting, and a green garden backdrop as you savor every course. Available for lunch and dinner with family-style and couple seating arrangements.

Private Celebrations

Corporate Events

Elevate your team dinners, product launches, and corporate gatherings to new heights — literally. Our versatile rooftop venue accommodates up to 150 guests with flexible seating, A/V equipment support, and bespoke catering packages.

Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Advance Payments

For certain private events, large group bookings, or special packages, we may require an advance payment or deposit. This deposit serves as a confirmation of your booking.

2. Refund Eligibility

Full refunds are provided if the cancellation is made at least 72 hours before the scheduled event or booking. A 50% refund is provided for cancellations made between 24 and 72 hours before the scheduled event. No refund is available for cancellations made less than 24 hours before the event or for no-shows.

3. Refund Process

Approved refunds will be processed within 7-10 business days and credited back to the original payment method. For any refund-related queries, please contact us at info@hotelhappystay.com.

4. Non-Refundable Items

Customized decorations, special arrangements, and perishable food items ordered in advance are non-refundable once the preparation has begun.
